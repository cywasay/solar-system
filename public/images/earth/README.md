# Earth atlas source imagery

These are measured, processed NASA composites, not AI-generated images or live feeds. The application uses Next Image responsive derivatives. Natural imagery is not recolored to match the page theme.

## Seasonal surface

- `january-2004.jpg`: 5,400 × 2,700 px, January 2004 Blue Marble Next Generation surface mosaic.
- `july-2004.jpg`: 5,400 × 2,700 px, July 2004 Blue Marble Next Generation surface mosaic.
- Credit: NASA Earth Observatory / Blue Marble Next Generation.
- Processing context: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/
- January original: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/january/world.200401.3x5400x2700.jpg
- July original: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/july/world.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg

Monthly, cloud-screened mosaics provide an aligned seasonal comparison. They are not individual photographs, same-day weather maps, or current conditions.

## Night lights

- `night-2016.png`: 5,760 × 3,240 px source graphic. The world map occupies the upper 2:1 portion; CSS crops the graphic footer without stretching the map. Attribution is reproduced in the interface.
- Credit: NASA/GSFC, Black Marble 2016. Image processing by Joshua Stevens, with science consultation from Miguel Román. Suomi NPP / VIIRS data.
- Dataset and processing notes: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/30876/
- Original: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a030000/a030800/a030876/BlackMarble_2016_global_7km.png

The night-light and seasonal surface datasets have different dates and processing. They are shown as separate observations, not simultaneous measurements. Night-light intensity is not a direct population or wealth measure.
