# Saturn image sources

- `pole.jpg` — Spring at the North Pole, PIA14945. Cassini natural-color view. [NASA Photojournal](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/spring-at-the-north-pole/)
- `titan.jpg` — Purple Haze, PIA06090. Cassini ultraviolet image with false color and brightened haze. [NASA Science](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/purple-haze/)
- `enceladus.jpg` — A Song of Ice and Light, PIA17144. Cassini visible-light image of Enceladus, its jets, the rings, and Pandora. [NASA Photojournal](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/a-song-of-ice-and-light/)
- `farewell.jpg` — A Farewell to Saturn, PIA17218. Cassini's last full mosaic of Saturn and its rings. [NASA Photojournal](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/a-farewell-to-saturn/)

Image credit for all four is NASA / JPL (Caltech) / Space Science Institute. These are width-limited derivatives served by NASA Science. The page’s ring projection uses the existing Saturn texture from the simulation. Its angle, minimum visible thickness, radial diagram, and final trajectory are illustrations.
