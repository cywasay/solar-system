# Mars imagery and references

These are sourced planetary observation composites and topographic data products from NASA, USGS, and ESA missions.

## Surface imagery

- `valles-marineris.jpg`: 2,400 × 851 px wide panorama composite of Valles Marineris.
- Credit: NASA / JPL-Caltech / USGS Astrogeology Science Center / ESA Mars Express.
- Context: Mosaic synthesized from Viking Orbiter and Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter (MRO) datasets, illustrating the grand chasm system of Valles Marineris (Candor, Ophir, and Melas Chasmata).
- The image represents natural and enhanced terrain reflectance to reveal structure; contrast is adjusted to highlight tectonic scarps and wall strata.

## Topography & Elevation Data

- Elevation profiles for Valles Marineris, Olympus Mons, and Jezero Crater are referenced to the Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter (MOLA) topographic datum (areoid), measured by NASA's Mars Global Surveyor (MGS).
- Data source: NASA Planetary Data System (PDS) Geosciences Node / USGS Astrogeology.
- Elevation scales are explicitly identified in the interface; vertical exaggeration is noted where applied for diagrammatic clarity.
